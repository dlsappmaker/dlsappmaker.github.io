Privacy Policy

Scanly – QR & Barcode · DLS Apps · Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information Scanly – QR & Barcode (the "app") handles and why. The app is free and supported by ads. It has no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Information kept on your device

Scan history and the QR codes you create (stored in a local database on your device). This information stays on your phone. We do not collect it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The camera is used only while the Scan screen is open, to read codes on your device. Camera images are never saved by the app or sent anywhere. Images you pick with "Scan from image" are read on the device and not uploaded.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, and information about how you interact with ads and the app. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Crash and diagnostic data

We do not run our own analytics. Google Play may share anonymous crash reports with us if you have allowed this on your phone.

Codes are read on your phone by Google ML Kit. ML Kit may send Google anonymous performance and diagnostic data, such as the device model, app version and error codes, to improve the service. It never sends camera images, pictures you choose, or the contents of codes. See ML Kit data disclosure.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your information. Apart from the advertising described above, the app does not send your information to anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
