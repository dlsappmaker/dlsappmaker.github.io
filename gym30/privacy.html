Privacy Policy

Gym 30: Knee & Back Plan · DLS Apps · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information Gym 30 (the "app") handles. The app is free. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Information kept on your device

Your ticks, pain log, weight entries, fitness test results and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not collect this information, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

The app has no internet permission. It does not send your information to us or to any server.

Backup file

If you export a backup of your progress, the file is saved only where you choose on your phone. We never receive it.

Permissions

The app uses vibration only, to alert you when a timer ends.

Pictures

All exercise pictures are original illustrations made for this app and bundled inside it. No outside photos are used.

Health information

The app gives general fitness information. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or physiotherapist.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Data sharing and selling

We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
